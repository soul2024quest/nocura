NOCURA / RESEARCH NOTES
Educational prototype for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

SCOPE
Two inputs: oxygen (13.5–21.9%) and airflow (0.106–16.298 cm/s).
Fixed baseline: 6.35 mm PMMA solid rod in microgravity stagnation-flame experiments.
The model was trained on 51 digitized experimental points.

RECORDED EVALUATION
Model version: 2.2.0-bass2-logflow.
11 held-out samples: 8 correct, 3 incorrect. Accuracy: 72.73%.
Confusion matrix (rows actual extinction, sustained; columns predicted extinction, sustained):
2, 3
0, 6
Small-sample results do not establish operational spacecraft fire-safety reliability.

OUTPUT BOUNDARIES
The predictor estimates sustained burning or extinction for the fixed specimen.
Its score is not a calibrated probability of a cabin fire.
The procedural 3D flame is a physics-inspired illustration, not a combustion solver.
Shape, colour, movement and temperature are not predicted measurements.
AI and fallback briefings are interpretations, not fire detection or certified crew procedures.
The ESA/NASA photograph is a fixed experimental reference; its source does not specify matching oxygen and airflow values.
